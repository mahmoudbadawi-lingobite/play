# World cover paintings (6 images, ChatGPT prompts)

Used on the world-select cards (the cards are wide and short, so the picture is cropped to roughly 2.3:1 to 4:1 around its middle).
Make each one **wide (landscape, 3:2 or wider)** and keep everything important in the **middle horizontal band**: the top and bottom
quarter of the picture may be cut off. No text, no logo, no numbers, no border.

Attach `adam.png` plus one screenshot of that world (or one of its object sheets) as the style reference for every request.
Send me the six pictures and I will crop and place them (`public/monster-quest/world/covers/world-1.jpg` ... `world-6.jpg`).
Until a file exists the card keeps its coloured gradient, so you can send them one by one.

## Style block (start EVERY request with this)

```
Polished mobile-game key art, glossy hand-painted look, clean cel shading, thin warm dark-brown outlines, rich saturated colours,
soft light from the top left, a friendly 2.5D storybook feel exactly like the attached reference screenshots.
WIDE panoramic composition (landscape, 3:2 or wider). Keep the important scene in the middle band; leave the top and bottom
quarter calm (sky or ground) because they will be cropped. The attached explorer boy may appear small (about 1/8 of the height)
walking on a path toward the scene, seen from behind. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no border, no frame.
```
